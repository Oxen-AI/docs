**Model ID:** `fish-audio-drama-3`

Fish Audio Drama 3 is a text to speech model built for performances you direct from inside the script, across more than 80 languages.

Directions in square brackets, such as `[barely holding it together]` or `[sighs]`, are acted out where they're written, and tag pairs like `&lt;whisper>…&lt;/whisper>` change how the words inside them are said. It suits character scenes, audio drama, and long-form narration. Language tags such as `&lt;french>…&lt;/french>` switch the language for a phrase, though they're still experimental. The weights are published under the Fish Audio Community License.
